
# My Favorite Series: Breaking Bad

## Introduction

My favorite series is **Breaking Bad**. It is a crime drama series about a chemistry teacher who gets involved in the illegal drug business. The story shows how his decisions change his life.

## Series Information

- **Title:** Breaking Bad
- **Genre:** Crime, Drama, Thriller
- **Creator:** Vince Gilligan
- **Release Year:** 2008
- **Main Character:** Walter White

## Why I Like This Series

I like this series because:
1. The story is interesting.
2. The characters have good development.
3. Every episode makes me curious about what happens next.

## My Favorite Characters

| Character | Role |
|---|---|
| Walter White | Chemistry Teacher |
| Jesse Pinkman | Walter's Former Student |
| Saul Goodman | Lawyer |

## My Favorite  Thought

> Sometimes, making decisions can change your entire life.

## Final Thoughts

Breaking Bad is a series that I would recommend to people who enjoy interesting stories and strong characters.

**Rating: 10/10**
